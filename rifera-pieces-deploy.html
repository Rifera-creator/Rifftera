RIFERA CURSOR PIECES — DEPLOY

Owner-only page. Deploy the Rifera Cursor Pieces NFT (RPIECE) on BNB Chain: 5 sequential pieces — Pointer, Ember, Verdant, Frost, Void — that build one First Cursor. Nothing here deploys or moves funds without your wallet confirmation. Do NOT deploy twice: one contract, one set of pieces.

$Cash math

  • 1 complete set = 0.008 BNB to the project wallet: piece 1 (Pointer) FREE, pieces 2–5 0.002 BNB each.
  • 100% of paid mint BNB goes straight to the owner in the same transaction. Nothing is kept in the contract (withdraw() exists only as a safety net for stuck dust).
  • Payment must be exact — underpayment and overpayment both revert. Always call nextMintQuote(wallet) first.
  • Max 5 pieces per wallet (one complete set, one of each, in order). No 6th mint, no duplicates.
