FAQ

What is Rifera Cursor Pieces?

A new NFT collection called RPIECE: one pixel arrow cursor split into 5 horizontal bands — Pointer, Ember, Verdant, Frost, and Void. Stacked together, the five bands form one whole cursor.

Can I mint yet?

Yes — mint is open. RPIECE is deployed at 0x55CcFC67FA2F631D95f82E0f37c59B03CBF4BAa8. The prompt on this page is ready to use now. Do not send BNB to any other address claiming to be RPIECE.

What is the total RPIECE supply?

Max 1,111 full sets: a cap of 1,111 pieces per type (Pointer, Ember, Verdant, Frost, Void), so 5,555 NFTs total max. Because minting must go in order starting from Pointer, if Pointer sells out, no new wallet can start minting. 3 pieces have been issued so far, all Pointer (totalMinted 3) — the numbers above are the cap, not the minted count.

How much does each piece cost?

Piece 1 Pointer is FREE (gas only). Pieces 2 Ember, 3 Verdant, 4 Frost, and 5 Void cost 0.002 BNB each. One full set totals 0.008 BNB, max 5 pieces per wallet, minting in order.

Why do I need an agent?

RPIECE minting requires a Binance Agent ID. You send the 4-line prompt, your agent calls the contract — there is no manual mint button for humans.

What is the Shard Rush game?

A free 100-round browser game with 4 styles rotating each round — RUSH (collect 5 pieces), DODGE (survive until time runs out; pieces give a score bonus and +5 seconds), CHASE (catch 5 fleeing pieces), BLITZ (30 seconds, collect as many pieces as you can, at least 5 to pass). Move the cursor (mouse, touch, or WASD) and dodge the glitches. The timer shrinks every 10 rounds from 60 to 30 seconds, and glitches grow in number and speed. 3 lives for the whole game, +1 every 20 rounds cleared (max 5). Clear round 100 to become CURSOR MASTER. No wallet needed to play, and game scores are not mint counts.

What is a full set for?

A full 5-piece set (Pointer + Ember + Verdant + Frost + Void) can be staked into the RiferaCursorStake contract: all five NFTs are locked into the contract and returned whole on unstake. The reward is BNB — every day 10% of the remaining pool is released and split evenly across all staked sets. The pool is funded manually by the owner from mint proceeds, cannot be withdrawn by the owner, and claims are open anytime. Staking is live at 0xeEBCC585b7783258A2B0E03A23367B92E6b75fdE, but the pool holds 0 BNB so far — rewards start once the owner funds the pool and sets are staked.
